The Atelier

From Sketch To Setting.

Every piece passes through the same three pairs of hands. It takes between three and nine weeks, and none of it is rushed.

  1. Design

    A pencil sketch becomes a 3D model. You approve the proportions and the stone before any gold is melted.

  2. Cast & Carve

    Recycled 18-carat gold is cast and then carved by hand. Each ring is shaped on the bench until it sits right on the finger.

  3. Set & Polish

    Stones are set under the loupe, claw by claw. A final polish brings the metal up to a mirror finish.
